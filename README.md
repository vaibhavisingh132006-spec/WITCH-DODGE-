# WITCH-DODGE-
In the fast-paced arcade game Witch Dodge, your goal is survival. Control a magic hat with the arrow keys and evade the relentless witch who chases you. Her power is fading! The witch loses one of her five hearts every 2.4 seconds. Dodge her until she runs out of life to win, but if she catches you, it's game over. Can you survive the chase?
